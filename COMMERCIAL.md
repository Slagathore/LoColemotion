# Commercial use

LoColemotion's commercial terms cover uses outside the Community License and
offer two rights options. Charles Chambers is the licensor.

| Option | What to discuss |
| --- | --- |
| Shared Improvements | Commercial permission with the agreed source-sharing obligations for covered SDK modifications |
| Private Modifications | Commercial permission to keep specified covered SDK modifications private, subject to the executed agreement and third-party rights |

Pricing is quoted by business size and the requested rights. The
[commercial agreement](licenses/COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md) and
[order form](licenses/COMMERCIAL-ORDER-FORM.md) define the transaction. Neither a
repository checkout nor an inquiry creates commercial rights.

For an initial inquiry, [open a licensing issue](https://github.com/Slagathore/LoColemotion/issues/new?template=licensing.yml)
with a non-confidential description of your integration and the rights you need.
We can establish a private contact route before discussing financial or customer
details. Do not post contracts or private revenue information in an issue.

If you are evaluating, read the [90-day evaluation terms](licenses/COMMUNITY-LICENSE.md)
and start with the [portable core](docs/GETTING_STARTED.md). If your interest is
specifically in the harness or material outside `sdk/`, say so: the current SDK
grant does not cover the whole repository.
