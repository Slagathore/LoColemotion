# Commercial use

LoColemotion's covered source is open source under the AGPL-3.0-only, and anyone
may use it commercially under those terms. A commercial agreement is the
alternative for uses that need other terms, most often shipping a closed
application that includes the SDK or keeping SDK changes private. Charles
Chambers is the licensor.

| Option | What to discuss |
| --- | --- |
| Shared Improvements | Ship closed applications that include the SDK, while sharing your covered SDK changes under the AGPL |
| Private Modifications | Also keep specified covered SDK changes private, subject to the executed agreement and third-party rights |

Pricing is quoted by business size and the requested rights. The
[commercial agreement](licenses/COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md) and
[order form](licenses/COMMERCIAL-ORDER-FORM.md) define the transaction. Neither a
repository checkout nor an inquiry creates commercial rights.

For an initial inquiry, [open a licensing issue](https://github.com/Slagathore/LoColemotion/issues/new?template=licensing.yml)
with a non-confidential description of your integration and the rights you need.
We can establish a private contact route before discussing financial or customer
details. Do not post contracts or private revenue information in an issue.

You can evaluate under the AGPL. Start with the [portable core](docs/GETTING_STARTED.md)
or the [harness guide](docs/HARNESS.md). The SDK and the covered lab, harness,
proof and replay use the same terms. The [root notice](LICENSE) lists the covered
paths and top-level runners. Simulation and training folders, `scripts/tools/`,
`docs/`, the editor, game and other unlisted material remain reserved unless
separately licensed. Mention any required material outside the grant in your
inquiry.
