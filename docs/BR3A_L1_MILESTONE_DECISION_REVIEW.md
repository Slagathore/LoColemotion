## Decision options

### Option A — accept the bounded milestone (recommended)

**Meaning:** accept only `BR3A_L1_ENGINE_CONTACT_TRUTH` at the exact report,
receipt, source commit, program scopes, and claim boundary above.

**Pros**

- The complete preregistered campaign is present; nothing was cherry-picked.
- Every run is independently source-, process-, artifact-, and receipt-bound.
- The failure history demonstrates that the pipeline actually refused invalid
  evidence instead of rubber-stamping it.
- BR1 remained unchanged and the experimental/accepted knowledge boundary
  remained closed.
- This closes the L1 engine/contact prerequisite needed before later loaded
  joint and support milestones may themselves seek promotion.

**Cons and consequences**

- The accepted truth is fixture-bounded. Later controller invariants may still
  require frame-level replay or new campaigns.
- There is still no per-foot load-allocation method.
- Articulated mass-ratio, joint force transmission, and load-bearing behavior
  remain future experiments.
- Acceptance requires a new append-only decision artifact whose evidence-basis
  schema names this BR3A report family; the existing BR1/BR2 decision shape
  must not be weakened or overloaded.

### Option B — defer

**Meaning:** leave BR3A formally open while requesting a specific additional
review or experiment.

**Pros**

- Preserves maximum caution if Cole wants to inspect selected raw capsules or
  independently reproduce a particular cell first.
- Allows a narrowly stated missing requirement to be added before acceptance.

**Cons**

- BR4/L2 and later support milestones remain unable to cite accepted BR3A L1
  truth at promotion time.
- An indefinite defer gains nothing: the complete declared campaign and its
  receipts already exist, so any defer should name a concrete unresolved risk.

### Option C — reject

**Meaning:** the present campaign is insufficient or its claim scope is wrong.

**Pros**

- Correct choice if Cole identifies a scientific or trust-chain flaw that
  invalidates the candidate milestone.

**Cons**

- Rejection should state the exact defective claim, fixture, artifact, or
  policy. A broad rejection without that information would not tell the next
  campaign what to repair.
