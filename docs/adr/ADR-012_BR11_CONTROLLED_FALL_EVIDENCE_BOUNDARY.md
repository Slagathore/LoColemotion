# ADR-012: BR11 Controlled-Fall Evidence Boundary

- Status: Accepted for commissioning and promotion-family construction
- Date: 2026-07-23
- Decision owner: Cole

## Context

BR10 established one scaffold-constrained reachable new-contact catch and
stance return. It did not establish what should happen when upright recovery
is no longer feasible. BR11 crosses a different boundary: one protective
link is deployed before core impact, motion severity is compared with a
same-state zero-command control, and both worlds are expected to finish
stably fallen rather than upright.

This distinction matters. A controller can reduce a preregistered mechanical
severity proxy without preventing the fall, predicting injury, allocating
load among body parts, or generalizing to another morphology, terrain, or
three-dimensional fall.

## Decision

BR11 receives a promotion family separate from BR10, BR1, milestone
decisions, knowledge admission, and future guidance consumers.

The family owns:

1. a strict campaign schema and fixed campaign document;
2. an exact clean-commit source inventory;
3. one metrics record and one evidence capsule per target process;
4. two fresh-process replicates of every declared program;
5. generic production bundle receipts;
6. a BR11-specific detached certification-report attestation domain;
7. one complete campaign report with independent bundle readback; and
8. an explicit boundary that certification is not acceptance, knowledge
   admission, creature repair, or guidance authorization.

The campaign fixes four milestone programs, BR11.0 through BR11.3, and one
integrity-only program, BR11.4. Each program executes twice in a fresh target
process. The complete campaign therefore contains:

- 10 evidence bundles;
- 122 milestone assertions;
- 86 integrity assertions; and
- 208 assertions total.

No declared run may be discarded. Reused target PIDs are permitted only for
non-overlapping completed invocations and must be recorded.

## Certified positive scope

BR11.0 certifies only:

- a strict semantic registry with one protective-distal and one core-impact
  role;
- classification from observed ground contact rather than desired contact;
- refusal of duplicate identity, self/creature contact, missing core role,
  nonfinite controller input, unknown phase, and digest mutation; and
- one pure finite PD request with no torque outside `FALL_ARREST` and no
  automatic-guidance authority.

BR11.1 certifies only:

- `FALL_ARREST` entry when upright recovery is infeasible, impact is
  imminent, and protective capacity is available;
- no supervisor force, torque, or guidance authority;
- a consecutive stable-observation dwell before `FALLEN`;
- dwell reset on instability and refusal of duplicate/nonmonotonic ticks; and
- honest missing-capacity and zero-capacity-control behavior.

BR11.2 certifies only:

- digest-bound paired initial-state identity;
- finite centroidal angular momentum and reconstructed external impulse;
- the preregistered whole-system kinetic-energy severity proxy;
- whole-system pre-core linear momentum;
- core approach speed and an explicitly local predicted core-load witness;
- explicit actuator-work, contact-dissipation, and mechanical-energy
  accounting; and
- fail-closed rejection of missing channels, false injury/allocation
  semantics, hidden control work, absent impact, late protection, unstable
  terminal motion, or unimproved preregistered comparisons.

BR11.3 certifies only:

- one exact planar two-body torso/protective-link scaffold;
- one finite paired unmotorized, unlimited hinge actuator;
- semantic protective contact before semantic core impact in the active
  world;
- one same-state zero-command Godot/Jolt control;
- improvement in the declared kinetic proxy, core approach speed, local
  core-load witness, and whole-system linear-momentum ratio;
- available angular momentum without asserting universal angular-magnitude
  reduction;
- finite external impulse and explicit energy accounts;
- complete paired receipts; and
- stable `FALLEN` terminal states without upright recovery in both worlds.

BR11.4 is integrity-only. It may reject weakened evidence but cannot replace
any of BR11.0 through BR11.3.

## Explicit non-claims

The campaign cannot establish:

- injury prediction or biomechanical injury tolerance;
- per-contact, per-body, per-foot, or per-toe measured load allocation;
- free-3D standing, balance, or bracing;
- an absent, negligible, or measured out-of-plane scaffold reaction;
- morphology, terrain, complex-foot, endurance, or articulated-limb
  generalization;
- generalized or transferred fall arrest;
- self-righting or getting up;
- gait or walking;
- accepted encyclopedia knowledge;
- creature repair; or
- automatic creature guidance.

Actuator saturation is permitted and recorded because it exposes finite
capacity; it is not silently discarded. The local core-load witness remains a
local predicted contact channel, and the kinetic severity proxy remains a
mechanics comparison rather than an injury model.

## Trust separation

Certification may produce evidence bundles, a report, and a detached report
receipt. It must not produce:

- a milestone decision;
- an accepted knowledge entry;
- a repair rule;
- a guidance policy; or
- a generalized fall-arrest claim.

Those operations remain append-only, independently reviewed transitions.
