# ADR-011: BR10 Reachable Catch Evidence Boundary

Status: accepted architecture; milestone acceptance remains a separate
decision.

## Context

BR10 crosses a materially different boundary from BR9. BR9 retained two
already-bearing contacts and redistributed commanded load. BR10 begins with
one bearing distal contact and one unloaded limb, plans a reachable target,
creates a genuinely new observed contact, transfers that observation through
`TOUCH`, `LOAD`, and `BEARING`, and returns the scaffolded body to a bounded
stance dwell.

That result is valuable, but it is still narrow. The rig uses the inherited
BR7 sagittal scaffold, a preparation-only root freeze, idealized paired finite
joint actuators, a fixed morphology, a fixed flat surface, one small declared
pitch impulse, and a development-only controller. Commands are not measured
per-foot loads.

## Decision

BR10 receives a promotion family separate from BR9, BR1, milestone decisions,
knowledge admission, and creature guidance.

The family owns:

1. a strict promotion-campaign schema and fixed campaign;
2. an exact clean-commit source inventory;
3. per-replicate metrics and evidence capsules;
4. generic production bundle receipts;
5. a BR10-specific detached certification-report attestation domain;
6. a complete report schema and independent readback verifier; and
7. adversarial promotion-contract tests.

The campaign fixes four milestone programs, BR10.0 through BR10.3, and one
integrity-only program, BR10.4. Every program runs twice in a fresh target
process. The fixed accounting is:

- 112 milestone assertions;
- 70 integrity assertions; and
- 182 assertions total.

No declared run may be discarded. Replicate assertion labels must reconcile
exactly, while raw physics transcripts are retained without requiring
byte-identical numeric observations.

## Positive claim boundary

The family may certify only:

- deterministic reachable-catch candidate selection with explicit collision,
  kinematic, timing, support-improvement, and assistance constraints;
- observation-authoritative `SEARCH -> TOUCH -> LOAD -> BEARING` ordering;
- a strict sagittal tangent/normal J-transpose command map;
- the inherited unpowered BR7 sagittal guide and passive-hinge fixture;
- one initially bearing left distal contact and one initially unloaded right
  distal catch limb;
- release of the preparation-only root freeze with one declared pitch impulse;
- the first catch command on the first post-disturbance observation;
- finite swing and post-catch commands through paired finite joint actuators;
- bounded commanded normal-load rate and friction-reserved tangent commands;
- complete command-ledger, executor, receipt, and contact-capacity
  reconciliation;
- measured support-interval expansion;
- a full-horizon return to the declared scaffolded stance dwell; and
- separation from the matched no-catch crash control.

The analyzer's complete claim boundary is copied verbatim into the campaign
and pinned as a schema constant. Promotion cannot silently broaden or rewrite
it.

## Explicit non-claims

The family cannot certify:

- per-contact, per-foot, or per-toe measured load allocation;
- free-3D standing, unconstrained balance, or unscaffolded bracing;
- an absent, negligible, or measured out-of-plane scaffold reaction;
- general articulated-limb, morphology, terrain, complex-foot, or endurance
  behavior;
- generalized fall arrest;
- self-righting or getting up;
- gait or walking;
- creature repair;
- accepted encyclopedia knowledge; or
- automatic creature guidance.

The local predicted normal-load witness remains a contact-quality instrument.
The L1.6-L1.8 transmission-blindness constraints still forbid treating raw
local impulses or summed collision-shape impulses as generalized foot load.

## Integrity role

BR10.4 may reject digest mutation, hidden authority, forged sensing, skipped
phase order, late command timing, support-prediction mismatch, bearing loss,
excessive command slew, unstable handoff, or an inadequate control. It cannot
substitute for planner, phase, force-map, or live-physics evidence.

## Consequences

A passing certification report is eligible for a later bounded human decision.
It does not make itself accepted knowledge. Observation admission, if later
authorized, must be a separate append-only operation. Any creature-guidance
consumer requires its own validated contract and explicit decision after
knowledge admission.
